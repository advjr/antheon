The E-Profile isn't a test project. It's an ongoing move in markets where professionals are expected to have a credible, lawful online presence, and where the law dictates exactly what that presence may say.

Hong Kong Singapore Australia Philippines · onboarding

In many countries, a professional's website is governed by law, especially in regulated fields like medicine and finance. There are strict rules on what may be advertised, what must be disclosed, and how a practitioner may present themselves to the public.

That's where Antheon's E-Profile comes in. We follow each country's governing laws and build the required content, disclaimers, and disclosures into your site, so it stays lawful while still reflecting your goals and requirements as the client.

  • Required legal disclaimers and disclosures included
  • Advertising and publicity rules respected by design
  • Privacy and data-handling notices where the law requires them
  • Your content and intent preserved, within the rules
Example, Singapore: strict rules govern what medical practitioners may publish online, covering how services are described and what claims must, or must not, appear. Antheon builds the E-Profile to meet them from day one, so it's compliant while still presenting you clearly and credibly.
Bringing this to the Philippines: as we onboard Filipino professionals, we build each E-Profile around the standards you already answer to, including PRC licensing for doctors and dentists, the relevant rules for lawyers and financial advisors, and the Data Privacy Act for how client information is handled, so your profile stays credible, professional, and ready as local requirements evolve.
