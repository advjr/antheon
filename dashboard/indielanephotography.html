1 September 2026 v1.2

🎨Enquiry form styling is on hold until launch, by design

You may notice the enquiry form on this proposal still looks like Studio Ninja's default style instead of matching the site's dark theme. This is intentional, not an oversight. The form connected to this proposal is the exact same form already live on your current website, so any color or font change made to it updates both places at once. We started a themed version to match the new site, then reverted it once we realized it was also changing how the form looks for visitors on your live site today, before you had approved the new design. We will properly theme the form as part of the final launch step, once the new site fully replaces the old one.

🎨Enquiry form index.html
  • UpdatedTried a dark, on-brand style for the enquiry form matching the rest of the site, then reverted it after confirming it also changed the form's look on your current live site.
  • PlannedThe enquiry form will be themed to match the new site's design as part of go-live, once the new site is the only version in use.
