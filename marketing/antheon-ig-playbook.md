# Antheon Instagram Playbook
*Built for: PH-based account, targeting AU/NZ business clients. Based on 2026 Instagram ranking data (Later, Metricool, Hootsuite).*

---

## 1. Pre-Post Checklist

Run through this every time, in order:

- [ ] **Format check** — Reel or carousel first. Single images only for quick announcements (they've lost ~22% reach vs 2025).
- [ ] **Hook is in the first line** — plain language, no throat-clearing, works even if the rest gets cut off by "more."
- [ ] **Caption keyword check** — first two lines contain the actual words an AU/NZ business owner would search (e.g. "CRM," "systems audit," "manual process") — this is now read by Instagram *and* indexed by Google/Bing.
- [ ] **Alt text filled in** — Advanced Settings → Accessibility, 10 seconds, describe the image plainly. Feeds the algorithm a clean signal of what the post is about.
- [ ] **CTA is a question, not a like-bait line** — "which one hits closest to home?" not "double-tap if you agree."
- [ ] **Hashtags: 1–2 max**, no geographic tags. Use `#BusinessAutomation` `#ProcessOptimization` or rotate in 1 close variant — never stack more than 2.
- [ ] **No watermarks** — if any clip touches CapCut/other apps, export clean.
- [ ] **Posting time set** — see table below.
- [ ] **You're free for the next 30–60 min** to reply to early comments. If not, reschedule — the first 3 days (and especially the first hour) decide most of a post's total reach.

## 4. Do / Don't

**Do:**
- Lead with a share-worthy or save-worthy idea (checklist, diagnostic, "which one are you")
- Ask questions that invite a real answer
- Reply to every comment in the first hour
- Keep Reels under 90 seconds even though the cap is 3 minutes
- Use carousels for anything list-like — they get 9x the saves of single images

**Don't:**
- Stack 5–10+ hashtags — correlates with *lower* reach in 2026 data
- Use "like if..." / "comment YES if..." phrasing — flagged as engagement bait
- Repost anything with a visible watermark from another app
- Use location-based hashtags (#PhilippinesBusiness) — narrows you to the wrong audience
- Buy followers — tanks engagement rate, which signals weak content to the algorithm

## 6. First 72 Hours Protocol

1. Post at your chosen window, stay active for 30–60 min.
2. Reply to every comment — fast replies are themselves a ranking signal.
3. Day 2–3: if a post is gaining traction, consider a follow-up Reel referencing it (riding momentum ranks higher).
4. Day 3+: if reach is flat, let it go. Apply the lesson to the next post rather than trying to revive this one.
5. Weekly: check Insights → Accounts Reached → top countries, and compare AU/NZ share over time to confirm the strategy is working geographically, not just in raw numbers.
