The approach

Keep the domain, drop the monthly hosting

Right now, Squarespace charges you every month for two separate things bundled together: the domain name, and the hosting that serves your site. We only need the first one.

Your domain stays where it is

Your domain remains registered with Squarespace and renews yearly, exactly as it does today. Nothing about your web address changes, and no email tied to it is disturbed.

Hosting moves to a free plan

The site itself is served from a hosting platform on its free tier. We simply point your Squarespace domain at it. The Squarespace website subscription is no longer needed.

Everything lives in your accounts

The hosting account, the code repository and the domain are all owned by you. Antheon builds inside them. You are never locked into us to keep your site online.

Please read this part carefully

This is a coded site, not a Squarespace or WordPress site

This is the single most important difference to understand before we begin, and we would rather be upfront about it now than have it be a surprise later.

You will not be able to edit the site yourself

With Squarespace or WordPress, you log into a dashboard and change text, swap a photo or add a section whenever you like. This build does not work that way. Your site will be written directly in code, which is what makes it fast, clean, secure and free to host, but it also means there is no admin dashboard to log into.

Any change to wording, images, pricing or page structure comes back to Antheon as a change request, and we make the update and redeploy the site for you. During your first month, that is completely free under the included retainer. After that, changes are handled either ad hoc or under an ongoing retainer, whichever suits you.

You mentioned that you rarely change your content once it is set, which is exactly the situation where a coded site makes the most sense. You are trading day-to-day editing convenience, which you have said you do not need, for speed, a much better looking site and no monthly hosting bill. If at any point you do want to edit content yourself, a page builder can be added on (see the add-ons section below).

Why we lean toward Vercel

For a site that may later want a booking form, a newsletter signup, a database or a self-editing page builder, Vercel gives the shortest path to each of those, and its preview links let you see and approve any change on a private URL before it reaches the public site. For a photography and brand-led site, that image handling and preview workflow matter.

Why Cloudflare is still a fine call

Cloudflare Pages is free, fast, reliable and secure, and for a mostly static 10 page site the day-to-day difference to you is small. If you prefer to stay with Cloudflare, we will build and deploy there with no change to the price, scope or timeline in this proposal.

The decision is yours and it is not permanent. Because the code lives in your own GitHub account, moving between Cloudflare and Vercel later is a short job, not a rebuild.
