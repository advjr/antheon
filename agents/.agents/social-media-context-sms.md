## Target Audience

- **Primary audience**: Owners and operators at medium-to-large businesses who are scaling past what manual processes and disconnected tools can handle. Existing client base spans exercise physiology, travel, construction, and health/wellness — service businesses with real operational complexity, not solo freelancers.
- **Pain points**: Duct-tape systems held together by workarounds; a CRM nobody updates because no one owns follow-up; manual handoffs between tools where leads fall through; assuming "we just need a new website" when the real problem is upstream of it
- **Goals**: Operations that run reliably as they scale, without hiring for every new bottleneck; systems that are documented and handed over, not a black box; less manual busywork for the team
- **Sophistication level**: Intermediate — business owners/ops leads, not necessarily technical. Speak plainly, not in dev jargon.
- **Where they hang out**: LinkedIn (B2B decision-makers evaluating credibility); Instagram (per the existing `marketing/antheon-ig-playbook.md`, the account is PH-based and specifically targeting AU/NZ business owners — best windows are 3-7pm PH and 10-11am PH)

## Voice & Tone

- **Voice adjectives**: Professional, convincing, credible, direct, grounded
- **Formality**: Professional, but plain-spoken — the existing site copy favors concrete language ("nobody owns follow-up," "duct-tape systems") over polished corporate-speak
- **Humor**: LinkedIn — none to minimal, stay authoritative and credible. Instagram — occasional, light, industry-relevant (AI news, automation/agency-life humor, relatable "which one hits closest to home" moments). Reference point given: catmemes.hub's tone is cheerful and unpretentious, not sarcastic or edgy — apply that *warmth and approachability*, not literal meme-page energy, since the goal on LinkedIn/IG both is to look "real and legit," not silly.
- **Phrases to use**: "systems audit," "operational backbone," "the process feeding it," "documented and handed over," "engineer the system," "one connected system"
- **Phrases to avoid**: "synergy," "leverage," "disruptive," "game-changer," "excited to announce," generic engagement-bait ("double-tap if you agree," "comment YES if...")

**Style reference given by user:**
- [web_pros](https://www.instagram.com/web_pros/) — energetic but professional, carousel-heavy, tech/AI-education-forward. Use as a model for *format and confidence*, not literal tone match (web_pros leans casual/emoji-heavy; Antheon should stay a notch more polished/credible).
- [catmemes.hub](https://www.instagram.com/catmemes.hub/) — sets the humor ceiling: light, warm, unpretentious. Antheon's humor should sit well below this on the silliness scale — think "knowing industry joke," not meme-page material.

## Content Pillars

1. **Case studies / real results** — before-and-after systems transformations for real clients (lead response time cut, manual steps removed, etc.)
2. **Systems & automation education** — why a CRM nobody updates isn't a CRM problem, myth-busting "we just need new software," what a systems audit actually looks like
3. **Website advice** — process-first thinking: a prettier website doesn't fix a broken process upstream of it
4. **AI news relevant to business operations** — practical, not hype-chasing; framed around what it means for how a business runs
5. **Light humor / memes** — occasional, tied to the automation/agency/business-systems world (Instagram only, used sparingly to humanize the brand — see Voice & Tone)

## Content Formats

- **Preferred formats**: Instagram — carousels (case studies, checklists, "5 signs" listicles), single graphics (methodology snapshots, pain-point callouts), Reels (behind-the-scenes, myth-busts). LinkedIn — standalone text posts, personal story + data-hook posts.
- **Per-platform preferences**: Instagram carousels get priority per the existing playbook (9x the saves of single images); Reels kept under 90 seconds even though the cap is 3 minutes.
- **Formats to avoid**: Engagement-bait phrasing ("like if...," "comment YES if..."), stacked hashtags (5-10+), reposts with visible watermarks from other apps, location-based hashtags (narrows Instagram reach to the wrong geography given the AU/NZ target).

## Anti-Patterns

- **Topics to avoid**: Competitor bashing/comparisons, partisan politics, personal life details, unverified or exaggerated client results
- **Tones to avoid**: Hype-y, "rise and grind" motivational-poster energy, preachy, buzzword-heavy corporate speak
- **Content types to avoid**: Engagement-bait phrasing, stacked/irrelevant hashtags, watermarked reposts, generic memes unrelated to the systems/automation/agency world, location-based hashtags on Instagram
