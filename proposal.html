Please read this

The one thing to know first

You will not be able to edit the site yourself

With Squarespace or WordPress you log into a dashboard and change things whenever you like. This site is written in code, which is what makes it fast, secure and free to host, but it means there is no dashboard to log into.

Any change to text, photos or pricing comes to us as a request and we update it for you. For your first month that is free. After that, changes are handled ad hoc or on a retainer, whichever suits you.

Since you rarely change your content, this suits you well. You are trading editing convenience you do not use for a better looking, faster site with no monthly bill. If you ever do want to edit it yourself, a page builder can be added later.
